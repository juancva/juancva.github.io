gmail-cli privacy policy

gmail-cli is a personal tool used only by its owner on his own computer to read his own mailboxes.

  • It requests read-only access (gmail.readonly). It cannot send, modify or delete mail.
  • Mail is read on demand, at the owner's request. The owner may have AI assistants he operates for his own use (such as Claude or Codex) read the retrieved messages to answer his questions or check his own work; those providers process that content under their own terms.
  • Mail data is not sold, not shared with anyone else, and not used for advertising. gmail-cli keeps no copy of it on any server.
  • OAuth tokens are stored only on the owner's computer and can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
